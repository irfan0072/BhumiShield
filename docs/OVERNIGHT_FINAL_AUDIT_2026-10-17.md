## Release boundaries

A real service still requires operator-controlled domain/TLS/hosting, verified official data sources and identity enrollment, authorization from government/banking/payment providers, adapter credentials and callbacks, recovery/SMS where offered, privacy/retention arrangements, monitoring, backup operation and accountable release sign-off. PostgreSQL and signing-key rotation are not deployment-certified. Existing demonstration records must not be represented as real title or institutional bank evidence. See PRODUCTION_RUNBOOK_2026-10-17.md.

Dynamic catalogue/fee controls do not by themselves satisfy the request that every public/help field and record ingestion workflow be administrable. Remaining unsupported scope must be listed specifically at handoff; never replace missing providers or controls with success placeholders.
